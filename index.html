How to Play

  1. A secret word hides on the stone tiles. Tap letters (or type) to guess.
  2. Right letter? Your climber jumps up a ledge. 🧗
  3. Wrong letter? The lava rises. 🌋 Don't let it reach you!
  4. Finish the word to reach the flag at the top.
  5. Guess right letters in a row to build a combo for bonus points.
  6. One letter in every word is secretly golden. Find it for +25 🪙.
  7. Spend coins on power-ups: 💡 Clue, ⛏️ Dig (reveal a letter), 🧹 Sweep (clear 3 wrong letters), 🧊 Cool (push the lava back).
  8. Watch for floating gems 💎. Tap one for free coins!
  9. Play the Daily Dash every day to build your streak, then share your result.
